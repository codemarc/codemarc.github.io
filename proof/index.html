Waypoint

Written fresh from the spec, not ported from the old code.

A clean-room order-taking core for a Toast-class full and quick service POS: orders, tender, kitchen display and money. Written fresh from a normative spec, integrating with BLT's gateway and payments adapters over APIs, codebases kept separate. Multi-tenant from the first migration, with row-level security isolating every location. Offline is not a claim: a timed thirty minute outage drill, three terminals, a hundred and fifty mutations, zero lost lines.

Read only, until it earns the right to write.
